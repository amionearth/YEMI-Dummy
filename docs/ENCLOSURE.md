# Luo Xiaohei monitor companion enclosure

Design target: a small black cat head that clips or magnetically mounts to a monitor, inspired by the compact “face-on-the-bezel” desk-companion form factor. Suggested head shell: 92 × 72 × 35 mm, with two 30 × 32 × 3 mm ear plates mounted on SG90 horns. Cut a 29 × 15 mm OLED eye window, a 22 mm touch-pad area on the forehead, a 10 mm acoustic port for the microphone, a lower-edge button hole, and a rear USB cable slot.

Place both servos low in the head with their horns pointing upward into the ears. Limit travel in firmware before final glue-up so the ears cannot strike the shell. Use a magnet plus thin steel monitor plate, or a weighted desk base. Add finished-build photos to `docs/photos/` before the demo.
