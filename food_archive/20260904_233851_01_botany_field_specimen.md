# Field Specimen Report: Lichen Symbiosis

Lichen is not a single organism, but a composite organism arising from algae or cyanobacteria living among filaments of multiple fungi species in a mutualistic relationship. 

The combined form has properties very different from the properties of its component organisms. Lichens come in many colors, sizes, and forms and are sometimes plant-like, but lichens are not plants. Lichens may have tiny, leafless branches (fruticose), flat leaf-like structures (foliose), or flakes that lie on the surface like peeling paint (crustose).
